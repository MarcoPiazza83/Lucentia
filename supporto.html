What to include

For a technical issue, please include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, app version, and a short description of what happened. Steps to reproduce the problem and an optional screenshot can help. Remove any private information before sending attachments.

Frequently asked questions

How do I start a journey?

Choose the Giant Mushroom Forest and tap Enter. Drag and release to launch the ball. After that, watch the journey unfold and hear each bounce become a note. Choose Guided, Balanced or Free to adjust how much help the ball receives.

I can’t hear the music.

Check your device’s media volume, the selected audio output, and the Sound panel inside Lucentia. If you use headphones, make sure they are connected. The music stops when you leave the app and resumes when you return.

Where are my saved paintings?

Open the Herbarium of Light from the Home screen, next to Enter. Journeys are not saved automatically: choose Save at the end of a journey. Saved journeys stay on that device, so they do not automatically appear on another iPhone or iPad.

How do I share a painting?

Use the sharing option for your journey and choose the app or destination from the system share sheet. A title or dedication may be included in the image, so review it before sharing.

How does Night Mode work?

Choose a duration in Night Mode and set your device down. New journeys launch automatically, the music gradually fades, and the session ends when the time is up.

How do I use binaural beats?

Connect headphones and choose Rest, Focus or Sleep in the Binaural Beats controls or Sound panel. This feature is optional and off until you select it. Binaural beats are a sound experience, not a medical treatment; follow the guidance shown in the app.

How can I adjust the visuals?

Use the visual preferences to adjust light and movement, including Reduce Motion. You can also change the forest’s atmosphere.

The world-release bell isn’t working.

You need an active iCloud account, an internet connection and notification permission. Check your device settings under Notifications → Lucentia. The bell requests a one-time alert when the selected world is released. Tap it again to cancel the subscription.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual journeys from the Herbarium. Deleting the app removes its local data; device backups and exported images must be managed separately. Cancel any world-alert subscriptions using the bell before deleting the app. For support-email deletion requests, contact marcopiazz.app@gmail.com. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Do I need an account?

No Lucentia account is required to play. Only the optional world-release notification feature requires an Apple iCloud account.
