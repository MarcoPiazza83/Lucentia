Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Your journeys stay yours. Lucentia does not require an app account. In the current version, your journeys and preferences are stored on your device, with no advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs or cross-app tracking.

Optional Apple notifications, emails you send to support, and visits to this website are explained below.

1. Who is responsible

Lucentia: Light & Calm is developed by Marco Piazza, the data controller for information handled to provide support. For privacy questions or requests, email marcopiazz.app@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves your chosen journeys, paintings, titles, dedications, records and settings locally to provide playback, the Herbarium of Light and your preferred experience. This content is not sent to the developer. The current version does not publish your journeys to a public leaderboard.

Local app data may be included in device backups if you have enabled them. Apple manages those backups according to your device and iCloud settings.

3. Optional world-release notifications

If you choose the bell for an upcoming world, Lucentia requests notification permission and creates a subscription using Apple CloudKit. This requires an active iCloud account. Apple processes the selected world, subscription information and account/device identifiers needed to deliver the alert. The app does not request your Apple Account name or email address from Apple.

The subscription is used only for the requested world-release alert. It is configured to fire once and then be removed. You can cancel it earlier by tapping the bell again while connected to iCloud. You can also disable notifications in your device settings; disabling delivery does not itself delete the CloudKit subscription.

4. Images you choose to share

A painting is exported only when you choose to share it. The image may contain the title or dedication you added. Check its contents before sharing. The receiving app or service handles the exported copy under its own privacy policy.

5. Contacting support

If you email support, I receive your email address, any name included in the message, its contents and any attachments you choose to send. This information is used to answer your request and troubleshoot the issue, not for advertising. Email is handled through Gmail, provided by Google. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

Support correspondence is processed on the basis of the legitimate interest in responding to requests and maintaining the app. It is kept only as long as needed to resolve and follow up on the request, or to meet applicable legal obligations. You may ask for its deletion at any time.

6. This website and service providers

This website does not add analytics, advertising trackers or cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. This website activity is separate from your local app journeys.

Apple provides the App Store, iCloud and notification services; Google handles support email; GitHub hosts these pages. Information is used only as needed for these services, and is not sold or shared with advertising networks. These providers process information under their own privacy terms and may process it outside your country, subject to their applicable transfer safeguards. Their security, retention and privacy practices are explained here:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Privacy Statement

7. Retention, deletion and your choices

  • Saved journeys: remain on your device until you delete them from the Herbarium or delete the app.
  • Preferences and other local app data: remove them by deleting the app, rather than offloading it. Copies in backups are managed separately through your Apple backup settings.
  • World alerts: cancel using the bell before deleting the app. Uninstalling the app does not itself request deletion of a CloudKit subscription.
  • Support emails: contact the address below to request access, correction or deletion of correspondence.
  • Shared images: manage exported copies in the app or service to which you sent them.

There is no Lucentia account to delete. The developer cannot remotely access or delete journeys that exist only on your device.

8. Your privacy rights

Where applicable, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of personal information held about you, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing. Contact marcopiazz.app@gmail.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority, including the Italian Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

9. Children

Lucentia does not request personal details from children or require registration. If you believe a child has sent personal information through support, contact me to request its removal.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated when the app’s data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Any newly required permissions will be requested before the relevant optional feature is enabled.
